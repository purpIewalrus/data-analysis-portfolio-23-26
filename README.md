### :newspaper_roll: [Rhododendron](https://github.com/purpIewalrus/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/rhododendron)
January 2026 | A quantitative study on the relationship between journalists' gender and media specialisation.

### :teddy_bear: [Automata & Mechanical Toys Market](https://github.com/purpIewalrus/data-analysis-portfolio/blob/main/automata-and-mechanical-toys-market)
January 2026 | A qualitative and quantitative study conducted on the market for automata and mechanical toys.

### :musical_score: [Marisa Ferrer 5](https://github.com/purpIewalrus/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/marisaferrer5)
May 2025 | A descriptive analysis that examines the canonical status of an operatic work based on its characteristics and its context of reception.

### :speaking_head: [Tonality & Speech](https://github.com/purpIewalrus/data-analysis-portfolio/blob/main/tonality-and-speech)
January 2025 | A study based on an experiment examining the link between language and relative pitch.

### :ship: [Titanic](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/titanic)

### :books: [Lightnovel Collection](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/lightnovels-collection)
June 2024 | A programme written for the purpose of collecting information about lightnovel works from the [lightnovelworld](https://www.lightnovelworld.com/hub_29071230#:~:text=Light%20Novel%20World%20is%20a%20very%20special%20platform%20where%20you) website.

### :art: [Art Students Sociology](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/art-students-sociology)
June 2024 | A descriptive analysis carried out from a questionnaire distributed to art students to determine whether their artistic practices are influenced by their social background.

### :meat_on_bone: [Cannibalism & Democracy](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/cannibalism-and-democracy)
April 2024 | A descriptive analysis that studies at whether a country's regime has an influence on cannibalism.

### :headstone: [Kenny's Deaths](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/kenny-s-deaths)
January 2024 | A descriptive analysis that explores whether the multiple deaths of Kenny's character in _South Park_ truly reflect the fears of American society.

### :ocean: [Flooding & Natation](https://github.com/eloisedelerue/data-analysis-portfolio/tree/main/flooding-and-natation)
November 2023 | A descriptive analysis that investigates the link between GDP, flooding and a country's Olympic swimming medals.
